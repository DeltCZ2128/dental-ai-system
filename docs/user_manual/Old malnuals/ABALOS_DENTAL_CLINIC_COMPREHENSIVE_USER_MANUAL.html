SECTION 1: PATIENT PORTAL USER MANUAL

Chapter 1: Overview & Access

The Patient Portal enables patients to self-register, manage their personal profile, view doctor availability, book appointments, and monitor their queue status online without requiring physical visits to the clinic.

Chapter 2: Account Registration & Email Verification

Open your web browser and navigate to the clinic registration page (register.php).

• Full Name & Contact Number: Enter your complete legal name and active 11-digit mobile number (e.g., 09171234567).

• Email Address: Input a valid email address. This will be used for account authentication and notification delivery.

• Password Security: Create a password containing at least 8 alphanumeric characters and confirm it.

• Click [Create Account].

• OTP Verification: Retrieve the 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered email address and enter it on the verification screen to activate your account.

Upon successful verification, your unique Patient ID (e.g., PAT-2026-0001) will be assigned.

  1. Personal Information Fields [Arrow 1 - Red]: Enter your First Name, Last Name, and active 11-digit mobile phone number.
  2. Email Address Field [Arrow 2 - Blue]: Provide your valid personal email for account activation and OTP dispatch.
  3. Contact Number Field [Arrow 3 - Purple]: Confirm your primary mobile contact number for schedule alerts.
  4. Password Setup [Arrow 4 - Green]: Enter and confirm a strong password with at least 8 characters, then submit.
Figure 1: Patient Account Registration Form with Step-by-Step Highlights

Chapter 2.1: Email One-Time Password (OTP) Verification

To protect patient accounts from automated submissions, the system requires Email OTP Verification before granting system access. A 6-digit code is dispatched immediately to your email address.

  1. 6-Digit Numeric Input [Arrow 1 - Red]: Type the 6-digit code received in your email inbox.
  2. Verify Account Button [Arrow 2 - Green]: Click to authenticate your credentials and generate your unique Patient ID (e.g., PAT-2026-0001).
  3. Resend Verification Code [Arrow 3 - Blue]: If code is not received within 60 seconds, click to issue a fresh OTP code.
[OTP EXPIRATION] Verification codes remain valid for 10 minutes. If not found in your inbox, check your Spam or Promotions folder.
Figure 1.1: 6-Digit Email OTP Account Verification Screen

Chapter 5: Appointment Booking Rules & Step-by-Step Procedure

Operational Constraints & Safety Rules:

• Daily Booking Limit: A maximum of two (2) appointments per day per patient account is enforced to prevent slot-hoarding and protect patient safety (avoiding procedure fatigue/anesthesia over-exposure).

• Lunch Break Exclusion: The 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM slot is automatically blocked across all scheduling calendars.

• Clinic Hours: Appointments can only be booked between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM (Monday to Saturday).

Booking Steps:

1. Click [Book Appointment] on the dashboard.

2. Select Date: Click an open date on the calendar (Sundays and full-capacity days are disabled).

3. Select Attending Dentist: Choose your preferred licensed practitioner from the dropdown list.

4. Select Dental Service: Select the required procedure (e.g., Dental Extraction, Cleaning, Consultation, Fillings/Pasta, Ortho Checkup).

5. Select Available Time Slot: Choose an open time slot generated dynamically based on estimated procedure duration.

6. Click [Confirm Booking]. Your booking request status will set to Pending Approval.

  1. Calendar Date Picker [Arrow 1 - Green]: Select an available working date (Sundays and closed days disabled).
  2. Attending Dentist Selector [Arrow 2 - Blue]: Choose your preferred doctor from the active clinical staff roster.
  3. Dental Service Selection [Arrow 3 - Purple]: Select the specific procedure (Extraction, Cleaning, Restoration, etc.).
  4. Time Slot & Confirm [Arrow 4 - Red]: Select an open operating hour (excluding 12 PM - 1 PM lunch break) and confirm.
[SAFETY CONSTRAINTS] The system automatically blocks 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM lunch breaks and caps active appointments to 2 per day per patient.
Figure 4: Dynamic Calendar Booking Interface

Chapter 6: Managing Bookings & Queue Monitoring

Navigate to [My Appointments] to view past and active bookings.

Status Indicators:

• Pending: Under review by clinic administration.

• Confirmed: Approved and scheduled.

• Checked-In / Serving: Patient has arrived at the clinic and is currently queued or receiving treatment.

• Completed: Session finished; consultation records saved.

• Cancelled / No-Show: Booking cancelled or marked unattended after the 30-minute grace period.

Contactless Reception Walk-In QR: Patients visiting without an appointment can scan the clinic desk QR code using a smartphone to receive an automated queue number (e.g., #Q-005) and monitor their turn in the lobby.

  1. Active Appointment Card [Arrow 1 - Blue]: Review appointment details, assigned dentist, procedure, and time.
  2. Status Indicator Badge [Arrow 2 - Green]: Displays live status (Pending, Confirmed, Checked-In / Serving, Completed, Cancelled / No-Show).

Chapter 2.1: 7-Day Machine Learning Demand Forecast

The integrated machine learning module applies a Linear Regression Algorithm ($y = mx + b$) trained on 90 days of historical booking data. It forecasts appointment volume for the next 7 operational days, classifying days into Low, Medium, or High demand.

The system generates Smart Reschedule Recommendations, allowing administrators to guide walk-in patients or reschedule non-urgent visits to low-demand days to optimize clinic throughput.

  1. 7-Day Forecast Table [Arrow 1 - Blue]: Review projected patient volumes, actual bookings, and demand levels (Low, Medium, High).
  2. Smart Reschedule Suggestions [Arrow 2 - Green]: Actionable recommendations routing flexible appointments to low-demand days.
Figure 10.1: 7-Day Linear Regression Demand Forecast & Workload Optimizer

Chapter 4: Emergency Patient Reassignment Protocol (Absent Dentist Handling)

When an attending dentist is marked as Absent or takes emergency leave on a date with scheduled appointments:

1. Open the affected appointment in [Master Appointments].

2. The system automatically detects doctor absence and displays a prominent [REASSIGN DENTIST] action alert.

3. Click [REASSIGN DENTIST] to open the dynamic reassignment modal.

4. Select an active, available dentist from the dropdown menu.

5. The system automatically executes real-time conflict-checking to ensure the target dentist:

• Is on duty on that day.

• Has no overlapping appointments or time conflicts.

6. Confirm reassignment. Notification emails/alerts are dispatched to both the newly assigned dentist and the patient.

  1. Doctor Absence Warning [Arrow 1 - Red]: Highlights that the assigned dentist is marked Absent on the appointment date.
  2. REASSIGN DENTIST Action Alert [Arrow 2 - Orange]: Prominent button displayed exclusively when assigned doctor is absent.
  3. Substitute Dentist Dropdown [Arrow 3 - Blue]: Select an available doctor verified free of schedule conflicts.
Figure 12: Emergency Doctor Absence & Dynamic Patient Reassignment Modal
