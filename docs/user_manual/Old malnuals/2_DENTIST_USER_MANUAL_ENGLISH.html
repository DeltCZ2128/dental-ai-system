SECTION 2: DENTIST PORTAL USER MANUAL

Chapter 1: Overview

The Dentist Portal provides attending dental practitioners with tools to review daily patient schedules, manage live treatment queues, access medical histories, and input clinical treatment notes.

Chapter 2: Dentist Dashboard & Daily Workload

Upon logging into the doctor account:

• Today's Patient Count: Displays total assigned appointments for the day.

• Active Waiting Queue: Real-time counter of patients checked in and waiting in the clinic lobby.

• Schedule Summary: Visual timeline of assigned procedures from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

  1. Today's Patient Count [Arrow 1 - Blue]: Aggregates total booked appointments assigned to the doctor for today.
  2. Active Waiting Queue Counter [Arrow 2 - Green]: Real-time counter of patients checked in and waiting in the lobby.
  3. Clinical Navigation Sidebar [Arrow 3 - Purple]: Quick access to Daily Schedule, Live Queue, and Rest Day Settings.

Chapter 3: Daily Patient Schedule & History Access

Navigate to [Daily Schedule].

Click [View Details] next to any assigned patient to open their comprehensive medical background, previous treatment history, and chief complaint.

  1. Daily Schedule Table [Arrow 1 - Blue]: Displays chronological schedule of booked patients from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
  2. Patient Information [Arrow 2 - Green]: Displays patient name, contact number, and booked dental procedure.
  3. View Details & Action Menu [Arrow 3 - Orange]: Open medical records, chief complaints, and historical treatment notes.
Figure 7: Daily Patient Schedule & Treatment Roster

Chapter 4: Live Queue Management & Consultation Charting

Select [Live Queue] to manage active clinic visits.

1. Click [Call / Serving] when ready to admit the next checked-in patient into the dental operatory chair.

2. During or immediately following treatment:

• Record clinical diagnosis, treatment performed, and prescribed medication.

• Input follow-up recommendations if applicable.

3. Click [Complete Session] to update the appointment status to Completed and release the queue slot.

  1. Waiting Queue List [Arrow 1 - Blue]: Real-time sequence of checked-in lobby patients awaiting operatory admission.
  2. Current Serving Chair [Arrow 2 - Orange]: Patient currently in chair with active session timer and diagnosis notes.
  3. Consultation Charting [Arrow 3 - Green]: Enter diagnosis, procedure notes, and click Complete Session.
Figure 8: Operatory Live Queue Management & Consultation Charting

Chapter 5: Rest Days & Leave Protocol

Dentists must coordinate planned time-off or emergency leave with the Clinic Administrator.

When marked as Absent or On Leave in the system, dynamic conflict-checking automatically prevents new bookings for that dentist and prompts administrative reassignment for existing bookings.

  1. Recurring Rest Day Selector [Arrow 1 - Blue]: Select recurring weekly off-day to automatically block booking availability.
  2. Scheduled Clinical Leave [Arrow 2 - Green]: Log planned conference or personal leave dates coordinated with administration.
