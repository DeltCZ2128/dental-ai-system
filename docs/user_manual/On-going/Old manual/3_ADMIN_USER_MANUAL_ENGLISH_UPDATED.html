Section 3: Administrator Portal User Manual

3.1 Master Administrative Control Overview & Security Access

The Administrator Portal serves as the operational nerve center of Abalos Dental Clinic. It grants clinic administrators, reception managers, and medical directors centralized control over patient appointments, practitioner rosters, dynamic queue pacing, AI-powered predictive demand forecasting, patient medical directories, official reporting, system security, and database backup routines.

The portal enforces strict role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure administrative features and patient records are guarded against unauthorized modification.

Operational Login Steps: 1. Navigate to the administrative portal sign-in page. 2. Enter your authorized Master Administrator credentials (Username/Email and Password). 3. Click [Login] to access the Executive Dashboard.

[Administrative Security]
Administrative accounts hold elevated privileges across the clinic database. Never leave administrative browser sessions unattended on public reception counters.

3.2 Executive Dashboard & Operational Analytics

Upon authentication, the Executive Dashboard presents real-time operational Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), summary metric counters, dynamic queue progress, and fast system navigation shortcuts across the entire facility:

  1. Operational KPI Cards (Arrow 1 - Blue): Master counters displaying Today's Total Appointments, Pending Approval, Confirmed, and Completed patient consultations.
  2. Real-Time Live Queue & Demand Forecast Quick Counters (Arrow 2 - Green): Live lobby waiting counter and weekly demand snapshot for immediate frontline coordination.
  3. Master Administrator Navigation Sidebar (Arrow 3 - Purple): Global navigation sidebar providing direct access to Dashboard, Appointments, AI Forecast, Dentists, Patients, Reports, Settings, and System Logs.

3.3 7-Day Machine Learning Demand Forecast (Linear Regression)

The system incorporates a 7-Day Machine Learning Demand Forecast Module powered by a Multi-Variable Linear Regression algorithm. It analyzes historical booking patterns—combining 60% general volume trend with 40% day-of-week historical averages (with Sunday strictly set to 0)—to predict upcoming daily patient volumes.

Operational Demand Tiers & Load Balancing:

• Low Demand (< 15 patients): Normal staffing and full booking availability. The system recommends scheduling lengthy, complex procedures (e.g., surgical impactions, multiple root canals, prosthodontics) on these days. • Medium Demand (15–25 patients): Moderate load; optimal resource distribution and standard chair occupancy. • High Demand (> 25 patients): Capacity threshold; the system alerts administrators and prompts smart rescheduling recommendations to shift non-urgent routine consultations or oral prophylaxis visits to lower-demand days.

  1. Date Range & Forecast Analytics Filter Controls (Arrow 1 - Blue): Date range pickers and analytical filters to examine historical patient influx trends.
  2. Historical Appointment Trend vs. 7-Day AI Demand Forecast Chart & Table Breakdown (Arrow 2 - Green): Visual trend lines, confidence intervals, and weekday patient volume projections used to balance chair occupancy.
[AI Machine Learning Core]
The 7-Day Demand Forecast uses Linear Regression combining 60% general volume trend with 40% day-of-week historical averages (Sundays strictly locked to 0) to optimize clinic staffing and load balancing.

3.4 Master Appointment Management & Status Lifecycle

Under [Appointments], administrators monitor, filter, and modify all patient bookings across their complete lifecycle.

Status Lifecycle Options:

• Pending: Awaiting administrative review and validation. • Confirmed: Validated and scheduled for dental care. • Checked-In / Serving: Patient has arrived and is currently in the clinic lobby or operatory chair. • Completed: Dental procedure finished and clinical record archived. • Cancelled: Terminated by patient or clinic with logged justification. • No-Show: Applied automatically or manually when a patient fails to arrive within the mandatory 30-minute grace period after their scheduled time.

Operational Steps: 1. Navigate to [Appointments] from the sidebar. 2. Use multi-status tab filters to view Pending, Confirmed, Today, or Past bookings. 3. Click [Approve / Confirm] to finalize a pending slot, [Reschedule] to modify dates/times, or [Cancel] to revoke an appointment.

  1. Multi-Status Appointment Filter Tabs & Search Bar (Arrow 1 - Blue): Status selector tabs and search bar allowing instant filtering by patient name or booking reference.
  2. Action Buttons Menu (Arrow 2 - Red): Comprehensive action controls on each booking row enabling administrators to [Approve], [Reschedule], [Cancel], or [Mark No-Show].
Figure 17: Master Appointments Management Interface

3.5 Emergency Doctor Absence & Dynamic Patient Reassignment Protocol

When an attending dentist calls in sick or files an emergency leave, the clinic administrator marks the clinician as Absent in the system.

Automated Reassignment Workflow: 1. Select the doctor filing an emergency leave in [Dentist Management]. 2. Click [Mark as Absent]. 3. The system automatically opens the Dynamic Patient Reassignment Modal, detecting all affected appointments assigned to that doctor for the day. 4. Choose an available duty dentist from the dropdown menu to instantly reassign affected patients without causing schedule overlaps or requiring manual phone calls.

  1. Doctor Absence Alert Warning Banner (Arrow 1 - Red): High-priority operational warning identifying the absent doctor and the count of scheduled patients requiring immediate chair reassignment.
  2. Affected Appointments & Cancel/Reschedule Control List (Arrow 2 - Orange): Scrollable roster displaying all affected bookings with patient names, procedures, and scheduled appointment times.
  3. Reassign Duty Dentist Selector and Mass-Update Action Button (Arrow 3 - Blue): Conflict-free dentist dropdown and execution button that transfers patients, updates chair queues, and dispatches SMS/email notices.
[Emergency Absence Protocol]
Dynamic reassignment prevents appointment cancellations during clinician illness, instantly migrating bookings to duty doctors with verified schedule availability.
Figure 18: Emergency Doctor Absence & Dynamic Reassignment Modal

  1. Active Dentist Directory & Credentials Roster Table (Arrow 1 - Blue): Central practitioner roster showing clinician names, license details, assigned operatories, and weekly rest days.
  2. Weekly Rest Day Configuration & Absence Logging Controls (Arrow 2 - Green): Shift configuration panel enabling administrators to set fixed rest days or approve leave requests.
  3. Add New Dentist Button (Arrow 3 - Red): Primary administrative control to onboard new dental practitioners and configure their portal login credentials.

3.8 Official Clinic Reports with Printable Letterhead & Signatures

Administrators can generate, filter, and print comprehensive operational reports formatted with official clinic branding.

3.9 Clinic Operational Settings & Business Rules Configuration

The [Settings] module allows administrators to configure core business rules governing the scheduling engine without modifying system source code.

Configurable Parameters:

• Operating Hours: Default set to 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Monday through Saturday (Sundays strictly locked). • Mandatory Lunch Break: Locked interval from 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM where bookings are automatically prevented. • Daily Booking Limit: Maximum of 2 active appointments per patient account per day. • Time Slot Intervals & Lead Times: Adjustable appointment slot durations (e.g., 30 or 60 minutes) and advance booking limits. • Service Management: Add, edit, or deactivate dental services and configure estimated procedure durations.

  1. Clinic Information & Operating Hours Panel (Arrow 1 - Blue): Controls for clinic name, location address, operating hours, and mandatory lunch break intervals.
  2. Business Rules, Lunch Break Locking, and Service Pricing Controls (Arrow 2 - Purple): Configuration controls for maximum daily bookings, procedural durations, and service catalogues.

3.10 System Database Backup & Disaster Recovery

To guarantee system security and data integrity, administrators can execute database maintenance and disaster recovery routines.

Operational Steps: 1. Backup Database: Click [Export SQL Backup] to generate and download a complete, timestamped `.sql` copy of the clinic database. 2. Restore Database: In case of emergency or server migration, choose a valid `.sql` backup file and click [Import & Restore].

  1. Export Database (.sql) Download Button (Arrow 1 - Blue): One-click utility that compiles all clinic schemas, patient records, and appointment tables into a downloadable SQL file.
  2. File Upload Selector & Import/Restore System Control (Arrow 2 - Green): Secure restoration interface allowing authorized personnel to restore database snapshots following system updates or server migrations.

3.12 Real-Time Clinic Queue & Walk-In Reception

The Front-Desk Queue Monitor consolidates both online scheduled patients who have checked in and walk-in clients who scanned the clinic's QR code into a unified waiting sequence.

Operational Steps: 1. Open [Reception Queue] from the sidebar. 2. Monitor real-time lobby check-ins and queue numbers. 3. Manually add walk-in patients if they cannot scan the QR code using their own smartphone. 4. Dispatch waiting patients to available operatory chairs as attending doctors finish preceding treatments.

  1. Combined Lobby Queue Sequence (Scheduled vs. QR Walk-In Badges) (Arrow 1 - Blue): Unified lobby display showing queue ticket numbers (e.g., #Q-005), patient categories, and current status.
  2. Add Manual Walk-In & Queue Status Action Controls (Arrow 2 - Green): Fast-entry intake modal and status control buttons ([Call], [Serve], [Complete]) to manage chair flow.

3.13 Administrative Troubleshooting & Technical Maintenance

Common administrative technical troubleshooting procedures:

• Overlapping Bookings: Verify dentist rest days and double-check operating hour constraints in [Settings]. • Unlocking Patient Accounts: Reset passwords or clear failed login attempts under [Patients]. • Weekly Maintenance: Perform regular `.sql` database exports every Saturday after closing (6:00 PM) to ensure reliable disaster recovery.
