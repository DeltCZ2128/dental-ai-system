Section 1: Patient Portal User Manual

1.1 Overview & System Access

The Patient Portal provides patients of Abalos Dental Clinic with a modern, self-service online gateway to register accounts, schedule dental appointments, review clinical booking histories, submit inquiries, and monitor live queue statuses in real time.

By enabling digital self-scheduling, the system eliminates prolonged waiting room queues, prevents procedural scheduling conflicts, and provides patients with transparent 24/7 access to dental healthcare services.

Technical & Browser Requirements: The portal is accessible on desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones using any standard, modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Apple Safari). An active internet connection is required to communicate with clinic scheduling servers.

[System Access]
Access the official clinic scheduling portal via the secure web domain provided by Abalos Dental Clinic. Ensure your browser is up to date to guarantee proper calendar rendering.

1.2 Account Registration & Password Standards

First-time patients must register an official user account before booking appointments. To create an account, open the portal login page and click the 'Register here' hyperlink located beneath the sign-in form.

Complete all required registration fields accurately to facilitate patient record matching:

  1. Full Legal Name (Arrow 1 - Blue): Enter your complete legal name (First Name, Middle Name, and Last Name) as it appears on government identification.
  2. Contact Number (Arrow 2 - Purple): Provide an active 11-digit Philippine mobile number (e.g., 09171234567) to receive automated SMS appointment reminders.
  3. Email Address (Arrow 3 - Orange): Enter a valid personal email address. This email serves as your primary login identifier, OTP verification channel, and electronic notice delivery address.
  4. Password Security Standards (Arrow 4 - Red): Create a strong, private password. The system strictly enforces the following complexity standard: the password must be between 8 and 16 characters in length and must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one numeric digit, and one special character (e.g., !@#$%^&*).
  5. Password Confirmation: Re-type your password in the confirmation field to prevent typing errors.
  6. Click the [Register] or [Create Account] button to submit your registration form.
Figure 1: Patient Account Registration Form with Step-by-Step Highlights

1.3 Email One-Time Password (OTP) Verification

To safeguard patient data and prevent unauthorized automated registrations, the clinic system incorporates mandatory Email One-Time Password (OTP) Verification.

Upon submitting the registration form, the server immediately generates a secure 6-digit numeric OTP and transmits it to your registered email address.

  1. Email OTP Input Fields (Arrow 1 - Blue): Open your email inbox, retrieve the 6-digit verification code, and enter the digits into the verification boxes on screen.
  2. Verify Account Button (Arrow 2 - Green): Click [Verify Account] to finalize registration. The OTP code remains valid for ten (10) minutes.
  3. Resend OTP Cooldown (Arrow 3 - Orange): If the verification email is not received within two minutes, check your Spam or Junk filter. Click [Resend OTP] after the 60-second countdown timer elapses.
  4. Upon successful activation, your official Patient ID (format: PAT-YYYY-XXXX, e.g., PAT-2026-0001) is generated and permanently linked to your medical profile.
[Verification Security]
Never share your 6-digit OTP with anyone. Clinic staff will never ask for your verification code or account password.
Figure 2: 6-Digit Email OTP Account Verification Screen

1.7 Step-by-Step Appointment Booking Procedure

Scheduling an appointment at Abalos Dental Clinic is fully automated and governed by intelligent clinical constraints to guarantee balanced chair utilization and patient safety.

Operational Constraints & Safety Rules Enforced:

• Daily Booking Limit: A strict limit of maximum two (2) appointments per day per patient account is enforced across the system. This rule prevents slot-hoarding, avoids excessive procedural fatigue, and ensures patient safety regarding local anesthesia thresholds.

• Operating Clinic Hours: Appointments can only be booked from Monday to Saturday between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays.

• Mandatory Lunch Break Exclusion: The 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM time slot is automatically blocked across all doctor schedules to ensure mandatory facility sanitation and clinical rest.

• Dynamic Procedure Time Allotment: Time slots dynamically allocate required chair durations based on procedure complexity (e.g., Consultation: 30 mins, Oral Prophylaxis: 45 mins, Tooth Restoration / Pasta: 45 mins, Dental Extraction: 60 mins).

  1. Interactive Calendar (Arrow 1 - Blue): Click an open date on the calendar. Sundays, clinic holidays, and fully-booked dates are automatically disabled (greyed out).
  2. Dental Service Selection (Arrow 2 - Purple): Choose your required clinical procedure (e.g., Dental Extraction, Cleaning / Oral Prophylaxis, Consultation, Dental Fillings / Pasta, Orthodontic Adjustment).
  3. Attending Dentist Selection (Arrow 3 - Green): Select your preferred licensed dental practitioner from the dropdown list. The system checks their active duty schedule in real time.
  4. Time Slot & Confirmation (Arrow 4 - Red): The scheduling engine dynamically generates open time blocks matching the practitioner's chair availability. Select your preferred start time, review the booking summary modal, and click [Confirm Booking].
[Booking Policy]
Each booking undergoes automated schedule conflict-checking to ensure no overlapping chair reservations occur across operating dentists.

1.8 Managing Bookings, Attendance Confirmation & Cancellation Protocol

The My Appointments module enables patients to track the lifecycle of their bookings, confirm physical attendance, and manage cancellations.

Appointment Status Lifecycle:

• Pending: Initial booking request submitted and undergoing administrative validation. • Confirmed: Booking verified by clinic administration; operatory chair reserved. • Serving: Patient called into the clinical operatory chair and currently undergoing treatment. • Completed: Procedure successfully finished with clinical charting archived. • Cancelled: Booking revoked by patient or administration prior to treatment. • No-Show: Patient failed to arrive within the mandatory 30-minute grace period.

  1. Filter Tabs & Search Controls (Arrow 1 - Blue): Filter your bookings across status categories (All, Confirmed, Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
  2. Action Buttons (Arrow 2 - Green): Use action controls to confirm attendance or execute self-service cancellation.
  3. Appointment Tracking Cards (Arrow 3 - Purple): Chronological cards displaying booking reference, attending doctor, procedure details, scheduled date/time, and live status.
  4. Self-Service Cancellation Policy: Patients may cancel upcoming appointments up to two (2) hours prior to the scheduled start time. Cancelling immediately releases the reserved operatory chair back into the public scheduling inventory.
  5. 30-Minute Grace Period Rule: Patients must check in at the clinic desk no later than thirty (30) minutes after their scheduled start time. Arrival beyond 30 minutes results in automatic marking as No-Show to prevent subsequent patient delays.
[Grace Period Rule]
Arriving more than 30 minutes late forfeits your reserved chair slot and marks the booking as No-Show. Please cancel in advance if you cannot arrive on time.

3.3 7-Day Machine Learning Demand Forecast (Linear Regression)

The system incorporates a 7-Day Machine Learning Demand Forecast Module powered by a Multi-Variable Linear Regression algorithm. It analyzes historical booking patterns—combining 60% general volume trend with 40% day-of-week historical averages (with Sunday strictly set to 0)—to predict upcoming daily patient volumes.

Operational Demand Tiers & Load Balancing:

• Low Demand (< 15 patients): Normal staffing and full booking availability. The system recommends scheduling lengthy, complex procedures (e.g., surgical impactions, multiple root canals, prosthodontics) on these days. • Medium Demand (15–25 patients): Moderate load; optimal resource distribution and standard chair occupancy. • High Demand (> 25 patients): Capacity threshold; the system alerts administrators and prompts smart rescheduling recommendations to shift non-urgent routine consultations or oral prophylaxis visits to lower-demand days.

  1. Date Range & Forecast Analytics Filter Controls (Arrow 1 - Blue): Date range pickers and analytical filters to examine historical patient influx trends.
  2. Historical Appointment Trend vs. 7-Day AI Demand Forecast Chart & Table Breakdown (Arrow 2 - Green): Visual trend lines, confidence intervals, and weekday patient volume projections used to balance chair occupancy.
[AI Machine Learning Core]
The 7-Day Demand Forecast uses Linear Regression combining 60% general volume trend with 40% day-of-week historical averages (Sundays strictly locked to 0) to optimize clinic staffing and load balancing.
