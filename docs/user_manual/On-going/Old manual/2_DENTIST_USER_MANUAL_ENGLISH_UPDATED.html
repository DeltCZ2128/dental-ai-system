Section 2: Dentist Portal User Manual

2.1 Overview & Practitioner Privileges

The Dentist Portal provides attending dental practitioners with comprehensive clinical tools to monitor daily consultation rosters, manage operatory chair queues, examine longitudinal patient medical records, and input chairside diagnostic charting.

The system synchronizes chairside operatory statuses with reception dashboards and patient notifications in real time, minimizing operatory idle time and eliminating manual paperwork.

[Practitioner Privacy]
All patient dental records, diagnostic histories, and consultation notes are strictly governed by Philippine Data Privacy Act standards. Attending dentists must safeguard their login credentials.

2.2 Dentist Login, Forgot Password & Logout

Attending dentists authenticate into the system using clinic-issued practitioner credentials.

Login Procedure: 1. Navigate to the clinic portal login page. 2. Enter your authorized practitioner email address and password. 3. Click [Login] to load your clinical dashboard.

Password Recovery: In the event of forgotten credentials, click 'Forgot Password?', enter your practitioner email, and follow the secure email OTP verification workflow to set a new 8–16 character password.

Session Termination (Logout): Always click [Logout] at the conclusion of clinical shifts or when stepping away from chairside computer terminals to protect patient medical confidentiality.

2.3 Dentist Clinical Dashboard Overview

Upon successful authentication, the Dentist Clinical Dashboard displays real-time operational summary metrics and required action items for the day:

  1. Today's Patient Workload Counter (Arrow 1 - Blue): Total confirmed appointments assigned to the doctor for the day.
  2. Active Lobby Waiting Queue Counter (Arrow 2 - Green): Real-time count of checked-in waiting patients seated in the clinic reception lounge.
  3. Clinical Navigation Sidebar (Arrow 3 - Purple): Direct shortcuts to Daily Schedule, Live Queue, Patient Records, and Rest Day Settings.
  4. Next Patient Card: Prominently highlights the next patient in line with their scheduled time, Patient ID, and requested procedure for rapid chairside preparation.

2.4 Daily Patient Schedule & Medical Record Access

Under the [Daily Schedule] menu, clinicians can review their chronological appointment roster from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (excluding the mandatory 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM lunch break).

Operational Steps:

  1. Chronological Appointment Roster Table (Arrow 1 - Blue): View assigned patient bookings organized chronologically across morning and afternoon operating blocks.
  2. Patient Summary Information (Arrow 2 - Green): Displays patient Full Name, Contact Number, Scheduled Time, and Procedure Requested.
  3. View Details & Medical History Action Button (Arrow 3 - Orange): Click [View Details] next to any patient entry to open their longitudinal electronic dental record, chief complaints, allergy history, and previous consultation notes prior to initiating treatment.
Figure 12: Daily Patient Schedule & Treatment Roster

2.5 Operatory Chair Queue & Consultation Charting

The Live Queue module governs chairside patient admission and treatment completion.

Operational Steps: 1. Navigate to [Live Queue]. 2. Click [Call / Serving] to call the next checked-in patient from the waiting lobby into the examination room. 3. Conduct the consultation and input clinical findings into the charting form. 4. Click [Complete Session] to update the appointment status to Completed and release the operatory chair for the next queued patient.

  1. Checked-In Lobby Waiting Queue Sequence (Arrow 1 - Blue): Real-time list of checked-in patients waiting in the clinic reception lounge.
  2. Active Operatory Chair Treatment Panel with Timer (Arrow 2 - Orange): Displays the patient currently undergoing active treatment in the operatory chair.
  3. Clinical Diagnosis Input Area and Complete Session Control (Arrow 3 - Green): Input Tooth Numbers Treated, Clinical Diagnosis, Treatment Administered (e.g., Restoration, Extraction, Prophylaxis), and Prescribed Medications, then finalize the visit.
Figure 13: Operatory Live Queue Management & Consultation Charting

2.6 Doctor Rest Days & Absence Protocol

Practitioners can configure recurring rest days or log planned leaves under [Settings] or [Profile].

• Recurring Rest Days: Select a primary weekly non-working day (e.g., Wednesday or Saturday). The scheduling engine automatically blocks patient bookings on that day.

• Emergency Absence / Sick Leave: When a doctor is marked as Absent or on unscheduled sick leave, the system locks their booking calendar and triggers emergency patient reassignment alerts on the Administrator Portal.

  1. Recurring Weekly Rest Day Selector (Arrow 1 - Blue): Select your primary weekly non-working day from the dropdown selector (e.g., Saturday - Standard Clinic Rest Day).
  2. Scheduled Clinical Leave Date Logging (Arrow 2 - Green): Log specific upcoming leave dates with description reasons (e.g., Academic Seminar, Medical Leave) to lock the appointment calendar in advance.
Figure 14: Rest Days & Clinical Leave Protocol Settings

2.7 Notifications, Profile Settings & Clinical Troubleshooting

Real-Time Clinical Alerts: Attending dentists receive instant in-portal notifications when new appointments are booked, when emergency patients are reassigned to their operatory chair, or when clinic administrative circulars are issued.

Profile Settings: Dentists can update their professional biography, dental specialty certifications, and login security credentials.

Troubleshooting Common Chairside Issues:

• Emergency Walk-in Priority: For urgent trauma or severe toothache walk-ins, reception coordinates with the dentist to insert an emergency triage ticket into the live queue. • Unstable Internet During Charting: The system caches form inputs locally in the browser. In the event of a momentary network drop, do not close the browser tab; click [Retry Submission] once connection is restored to preserve consultation notes.
